Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 23, 2026

1. Who we are

Kirsh Tours is a travel agency based in Petah Tikva, Israel. This policy explains how our app handles data received from TikTok.

2. What data we access

The app connects only to the Kirsh Tours TikTok account. Through TikTok's official APIs it accesses:

  • Basic profile information of our own account (display name, username, avatar)
  • Statistics of our own account (followers, likes, video count)
  • The list of our own public videos and their view, like, comment and share counts

We do not access, collect or store data about other TikTok users.

3. How we use the data

The data is used only to publish our own travel deal videos and to measure how our own content performs.

4. Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share TikTok data with third parties. Data is processed through our integration provider (Composio) solely to operate the connection.

5. Storage and security

Access tokens are stored securely by our integration provider and are never published. Statistics may be kept in internal reports of Kirsh Tours.

6. Your choices

The connection can be revoked at any time from the TikTok app settings (Security > Manage app permissions).

7. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The updated version will be published at this address.
